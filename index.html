How we work

Every collaboration is tailor-made

ABA practices don’t run the same way — different regions, payers, settings, and teams. We don’t sell a fixed package. We start by understanding how you operate, then design the reporting and automations around your needs, your systems, and the decisions you need to make.

Built around your questions

What’s actually happening with authorizations? Where is time being lost? Which regions or settings are thriving? We shape the analysis around the questions leadership is asking — not a template.

Fitted to your workflows

Your EHR, your payroll rules, your targets, your regions. The systems we build plug into how your team already works, so adoption is natural and the numbers are trusted.

Grows with you

Start with the most pressing need — payroll, utilization, provider follow-up, or leadership visibility — and expand from there. Each piece is designed to suit where your practice is now and where it’s headed.

In practice — Total Care ABA

A successful collaboration with Total Care ABA

Our work with Total Care ABA shows what tailor-made looks like in a real, multi-region ABA operation — practical automations and clear visibility that help teams act sooner and lead with confidence.

Working closely with Total Care ABA, we built systems around their specific operations — not generic reports. The result is less manual chasing, cleaner payroll, and a leadership view that makes it easy to see what’s working, where, and in which type of service.

Automated payroll

We automated Total Care ABA’s payroll process — pulling together worked hours and pay logic into a reliable, repeatable flow. What was once manual reconciliation became a clean, consistent process the team can trust each cycle.

Authorized vs. billed — in depth

Deep-dive views into client authorized vs. billed time, so teams can see utilization clearly: what was authorized, what was delivered and billed, and where hours are being left unused or need attention — by client, provider, and region.

Automated follow-up on targets

Automated chasing for providers who aren’t hitting their targets — timely flags and follow-ups without managers having to hunt through spreadsheets. Issues surface early, so support and accountability happen while there’s still time to act.

High-level dashboards for the whole business

Beyond the day-to-day, we provided high-level business and region dashboards that show the health and success of the operation at a glance — and let leaders drill from the overall picture down to what’s driving it.

  • Business-wide view: a clear, high-level picture leadership can rely on, without waiting for manual reports
  • By region: compare performance across regions and spot where things are strong or need support
  • By type of service: see the success of each setting side by side — in-home, clinic, and other service types — so decisions reflect how each part of the practice is really doing
  • From summary to detail: start high-level, then follow the numbers into authorized vs. billed, provider targets, and payroll when you need the why

FAQ

Questions owners ask first

Do you need access to PHI? ＋

We work with de-identified or limited datasets wherever possible, and we’re happy to sign your BAA / NDA before touching any system. Dashboards are built inside your environment or with access you control — you can revoke it anytime.

Which systems do you work with? ＋

CentralReach, Office Ally, and most EHR/billing exports (CSV/Excel). Our backend and automation run fully on Google Cloud Platform, so your pipelines are reliable, secure, and always on. For the frontend — dashboards and reporting — we’re flexible: we use whichever tool best fits your team, whether that’s Power BI, Looker Studio, Google Sheets, Excel, or something you already have. If you can export it, we can automate it.

How is billing handled if you’re Ark Analytics? ＋

Ark Analytics is the brand. Contracts and invoices run through Kavana Insights LLC — same team, clean paperwork for your AP department.

How does a collaboration start? ＋

With a conversation. Tell us how your practice runs and what you need clearer visibility on — payroll, authorized vs. billed time, provider targets, or leadership reporting. From there we design a tailor-made plan around your systems and goals, like we did with Total Care ABA.

Can you work with our existing biller / ops team? ＋

Yes — that’s the normal setup. We build the visibility layer and automations; your team keeps running billing and care delivery with better information and fewer manual steps.
